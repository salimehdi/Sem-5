Timesheet Application (Employee Management System)

Problem Statement
- The objective is to develop a Timesheet Application that enables efficient management and tracking of employee work for a company. The application will cater to three types of users: Managers, Employees, and Administrators.

User Roles and Responsibilities:-

Manager:
- Approve or reject leave applications.
- Generate and view work reports for individual employees or groups.
- Access and review timesheets submitted by employees under their supervision.
- Approve or reject submitted timesheets.

Employee:
- Apply for leave.
- Fill out and submit timesheets to their respective managers.

Admin:
- Manage employee credentials.
- Determine and configure organizational hierarchy.
- Add and delete employees from the system.
